# Content voice

The Studio is a **welcoming expert**: the senior craftsperson who greets you
at the door, shows you the workshop, and tells you plainly what something
costs and how long it takes. Direct, warm, plain-spoken. It explains like a
person, not a brochure.

## Tone in one paragraph

Say what the customer gets, in the fewest true words. Name things. Use
numbers. Stop when the sentence is done. Never sell, exclaim, hedge, or
hide behind jargon. In tools it is terse and consistent; in marketing it is
confident and short; in errors it is calm and tells you what to do next.

## Mechanics

- Sentence case everywhere: headings, labels, buttons, nav, badges (the
  mono uppercase is a style; the string is written in sentence case).
- Punctuation: periods and commas. No em-dashes, no semicolons in UI, no
  exclamation marks, no Oxford comma, no ellipsis except a literal
  `Loading…` for waits over 3 s.
- Numbers as digits (`5 practices`, `3 days`); spelled out only at sentence
  start. Tabular figures in columns and totals.
- `you` for the reader, `we` for the Studio, `it` for a product or practice.
  The studio name is written once the name is chosen — until then,
  "the Studio".
- **Banned words:** leverage, seamless, cutting-edge, world-class, next-gen,
  revolutionise, empower, unlock, robust, innovative, solution (for
  software), synergy, delight, effortless, powerful, smart (as a feature
  adjective), simply, just (as minimisers), ecosystem (unless it is one).
- **Claims** are limited to what the source material supports. No invented
  numbers, clients, testimonials, or ratings.

## Microcopy by element

| Element | Rule | Example |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Page title | Noun or noun phrase, ≤ 4 words | `Our work`, `Voice agents` |
| Kicker | Category or context, ≤ 3 words | `Wavia`, `E-commerce` |
| Hero headline | What the customer gets, ≤ 3 lines | `A voice agent that answers when your customers call.` |
| Button | Verb + object; says what happens; ≤ 4 words | `Book a call`, `See our work`, `Start a project` |
| Link | The destination | `Pricing`, not `Click here` |
| Field label | Noun; `(optional)` on the minority | `Work email`, `Notes (optional)` |
| Placeholder | An example, never the label | `you@company.com` |
| Helper text | One sentence, the why | `We reply within one working day.` |
| Error (field) | What is wrong, then what to do; no blame | `Add a work email we can reply to.` |
| Error (system) | What happened, what we are doing, what you can do | `The form did not send. Your answers are kept — try again.` |
| Empty state | What will be here, how it appears; one CTA | `No projects yet. Yours could be the first.` · `Start a project` |
| Confirmation | Name the thing; buttons are the outcomes | `Delete this draft?` · `Delete draft` / `Keep it` |
| Toast (success) | Past tense, the thing | `Saved`, `Call booked` |
| Badge / status | One or two words | `New`, `In progress`, `Live` |
| Tooltip | The control's name or a one-line fact | `Sort by date` |
| 404 | `We could not find that page.` + the one link home | |
| 500 | `Something broke on our side. It has been logged. Try again in a minute.` | |

## Before / after (voice calibration)

| Don't | Do |
|:--|:--|
| Leverage our cutting-edge AI solutions to unlock seamless customer experiences. | Voice agents that answer when your customers call. |
| Submit your details and our team will reach out shortly! | Tell us about the project. We reply within one working day. |
| Oops! Something went wrong. | The page did not load. Try again — your work is saved. |
| Our world-class e-commerce solutions | Your store, online and selling in weeks. |
| Invalid input. | Add a phone number we can reach you on. |
